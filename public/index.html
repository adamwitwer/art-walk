Take the guide with you

On iPhone, open this site in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Once the offline guide is ready, the stop list and artist details work without a connection. Street maps, artist websites, and directions need internet.

Keep track as you go

Save stops with the heart and mark them visited. Your progress stays in this browser on this device. These checkmarks are personal notes; they don’t replace the event’s physical passport stickers.

Accessibility

Six stops have a wheelchair symbol in the pamphlet. “Marked accessible” shows those stops. The guide doesn’t describe their specific access arrangements or the access at other stops.
